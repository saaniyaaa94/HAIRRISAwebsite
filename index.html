About the salon

A little slice of calm at Crater View Farm

Set on Runciman Road in Pukekohe East, HAiR at Crater View Farm is a friendly neighbourhood salon that's earned a loyal following — 4.9 stars on Google. Whether you're popping in for a quick trim or booking in for something special, expect warm hospitality and hair that feels like you, only better.
