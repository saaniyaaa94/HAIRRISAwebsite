About the salon

A little slice of calm in the CBD

Nestled inside the Atrium on Elliott Shopping Centre on Albert Street, Risa Hair is a friendly neighbourhood salon that's earned a loyal following — 4.9 stars on Google and a perfect 5/5 on Facebook.
